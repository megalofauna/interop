Chips Pattern

Also known as filter chips, choice chips, pills.

A pattern: a template of the checkbox group and the radio group. A chip is a checkbox or a radio button in its label, drawn as a pill. A value the user removes is a tag, not a chip.

Several choices

interop-checkbox-group="chips" draws each option as a pill: filter chips. A checked chip fills and shows a check, so the state does not rely on color. itx-size on the fieldset makes them smaller or larger.

One choice

interop-radio-group="chips" draws each option as a pill that sizes to its text and wraps. Use it for many short options. For a short row of equal options, use the segmented control.
