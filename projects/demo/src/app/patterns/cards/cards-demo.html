Cards Pattern

Also known as selectable cards, card selection, tile picker.

A pattern: a template of the radio group and the checkbox group. Under it each group is the same group, with the same keys, checked mark, focus ring, disabled state and audit rules.

One choice

Each option is a card. The input names the option with aria-labelledby pointing at its title and describes it with aria-describedby, so a screen reader reads "Pro, radio button, 2 of 3" and then the details, not the whole card on every arrow press.
