Badge

As text

A badge of text has a border in its color, a faint tint of it behind the text, and text that leans toward it. itx-color takes a role, info, success, warning or danger, or any CSS color. A custom color is the border, so keep it 3:1 against the page in both schemes.

Sizes

As an announced count

[count] shows the number and [label] says what it counts. announce speaks each change; give it to one badge per count.

As a status dot

A dot shows no text. Its text is still spoken, so the button reads "Activity, New activity".

Positions

itx-badge-position places a badge on a corner of its parent, half outside it. The corners are logical, so they follow right to left.
