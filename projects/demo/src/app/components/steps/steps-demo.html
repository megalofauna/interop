Steps

Also known as stepper, step indicator, progress indicator, progress tracker, progress steps, wizard, multi-step form.

A process split into ordered steps has two parts: a step indicator that shows where you are, and the steps themselves. The indicator is an ordered list with the current step marked aria-current="step". It shows progress; it is not a control. If the sections can be visited in any order, use tabs instead. A +/- number control, which some systems call a stepper, is an input type="number".

Step indicator

Every step before the current one is drawn as completed, with no attribute of its own. A completed step may link back to review it. A future step is never a link or a button. Write each status in visually hidden text, and put "Step 3 of 5" in the page title and the step's heading.

A multi-step form

One form interop-steps-form, with a fieldset per step. Each step holds its own buttons, which send --next, --back or --show-step to the form as invoker commands. Hidden steps are hidden, never disabled, so every value submits. Next checks only the current step. Enter in a field means Next. After a step change, focus moves to the step's heading. Without script, every step shows, as one long form.
