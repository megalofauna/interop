Slider

Also known as range, range input, range slider.

A slider sets one value from a range when the range matters more than the exact number: volume, brightness, zoom. It is the native <input type="range">. The browser gives it the arrow keys, Page Up and Page Down, Home and End, dragging, a click anywhere on the track, form submission and right-to-left. Interop draws the track, the fill and the thumb, and the house focus ring. The progress bar draws the same track.

For an exact number, use a text field: a slider is hard to set to one value. For a few named choices, use a radio group, which shows every option.

One value

Label it like any field. The whole slider is the target for the pointer: a press anywhere on it moves the thumb there.

Sizes

itx-size is sm, lg or nothing for the default. The slider is 24, 32 and 40px tall, its thumb 16, 20 and 28px, and its track 4, 6 and 8px thick. The small size keeps the 24px target WCAG asks for.

A value with a unit

format, an option of bindSlider(), turns the number into words for screen readers, as aria-valuetext: "$200", not "200". Use it only when the number alone misleads. readout shows the same words beside the slider, hidden from screen readers because the slider already says its value.

Vertical

itx-orientation="vertical" stands it upright, with the minimum at the bottom. Arrow Up raises the value, and screen readers hear a vertical slider.

Disabled

In a form

The slider submits its value under its name. Reset puts the thumb and the fill back.

Two values

A minimum and a maximum are two text fields in a fieldset, not a slider with two thumbs. People set exact numbers in fields faster and more reliably, and each field has its own label.
