Scroll area

A scroll area shows an inner shadow at each edge with content beyond it. The shadow darkens as more content is hidden on that side and fades as the last of it comes into view. When nothing inside can take focus, the area takes focus itself, so the keyboard can scroll it.

Firefox does not support scroll-driven animations yet, so it shows no shadows. Everything else works there.
