error, warning and loading resolve to duration: 0 — they persist until dismissed, and so are given a close button automatically.

observe() and promise() open a loading toast and patch it into success or error when the work settles. The success and error messages may be strings or factories receiving the resolved value or the thrown error.

cancelBehavior decides what dismissing the loading toast does to the work behind it: 'detach' (the default) lets it finish, 'unsubscribe' cancels the subscription, and 'prevent' refuses to let the user dismiss it at all — dismissAll() still can.

The rule underneath both defaults: anything that will not leave on its own must offer a way out, and anything that will should not add a button for it. So a plain six-second success toast has no close button, and that is correct — pass dismissible: true when you want one anyway.
