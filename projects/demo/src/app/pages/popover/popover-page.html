Anchored panel built on the native HTML popover attribute. Top-layer promotion, light-dismiss, and Escape are browser-native; placement is delegated to the same position-strategy infrastructure as the tooltip. Role is the consumer's decision — drop in a listbox, a form, or arbitrary content.

The trigger and the panel are decoupled in the DOM: give the panel a template ref with #ref="interopPopover", then point any focusable element at it with [interop-popover-trigger]="ref". The trigger wires popovertarget, aria-expanded and aria-controls for you.

This is a popover. Click the trigger again or press Esc to dismiss.

A 12 × 6 caret, drawn as two stacked triangles so the panel's 1px frame runs continuously around it.

Consumer-supplied [interop-popover-arrow] wins over the built-in caret. The structural CSS rotates it automatically per resolved placement, and pulls it out by its own size — so any icon dimension lands flush against the panel edge.

Twelve placements. The position strategy flips and shifts when the preferred side would overflow the viewport, and publishes the result on [data-placement] — which is what re-points the caret.
