Why it is built this way

HTML-first

Attribute selectors attach to native elements. <button interop-button> keeps keyboard behaviour, ARIA role, form participation and focus management for free — none of it needs recreating.

Angular-forward

Signals are the reactive primitive — no subjects, no manual change detection, no markForCheck(). Every component is standalone, OnPush and uses the new control flow. Not aspirationally. Actually.

Ships only what you use

Import InteropButton and you pay for InteropButton. Every component, icon and adapter is tree-shakeable by design; heavy dependencies like highlighting grammars load on demand.

Tokens that compound

Override --itx-control-accent at any ancestor and every form control in that subtree updates — checkbox, radio and toggle at once. No specificity battles. The cascade does the work.
