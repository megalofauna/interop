In-page navigation for long documents. Renders a list of anchors in either orientation, marks the current one, and optionally pins itself to the top of its scroll container.

The nav renders state rather than tracking it. You pass activeHref from your own scroll-spy, which keeps the component free of an observer it would have to guess the root for. Every demo page in this app uses one — the nav at the top of this page is the component documenting itself.

Vertical adds a visible heading, one level of nesting, and a dot marking the active item. Children render in this orientation only.
