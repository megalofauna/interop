Radius (comparison)

Every row renders the components at one candidate house radius, and the itx-radius scale it produces. The house radius is 6px with squircle corners; this page compares it with the alternatives.

  • Shape-defined corners stay round at every radius: badge, switch, radio.
  • Container corners take the house radius: button, popover, the segmented frame.
  • Small corners take half of it: tooltip, and the checkbox box, capped at a quarter of its size.
  • Nested corners take the outer radius less the gap, never below zero: the segmented pill.
Corner shape

Squircle corners need corner-shape, which only Chromium supports. Other engines draw round corners for both.
